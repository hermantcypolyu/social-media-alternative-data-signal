## 3. Claim-language audit

Searched the whole repository for `profit|profitable|alpha|predictive|significant|
outperform|risk-adjusted|sharpe|edge|generates return` and separately for
`proves that|demonstrates that|confirms that|real alpha|genuine alpha`.

**Every hit falls into one of four benign categories:**

1. **Explicit negation.** "not a return, alpha, profitability or economic
   significance claim" (`solution.md` §1); "It does not independently validate the
   annotation rubric, and it does not establish investment predictability" (nb02,
   printed output and prose).
2. **The mathematical symbol α.** `capped_attention(views, alpha=0.20)` — a
   parameter name, not a performance claim.
3. **A reported negative result.** "TF-IDF did not outperform the majority
   baseline. Δ = −0.0003" — a failure, retained deliberately.
4. **A statement of what is *not* claimed.** "Any reviewer who wanted a Sharpe
   ratio would have to accept one manufactured from data that cannot produce it."

**Zero hits assert a profitable or predictive result.** The single p-value
(p = 0.0050) is described in three separate places as a label-recovery result, and
§12 states: *"The p-value concerns label recovery, nothing else."*

## 6. Remaining limitations

These are stated in the report and are **not** defects to be fixed — they are
honest bounds on what the work supports.

1. **Single annotator, no inter-annotator agreement.** The 0.7251 macro F1 measures
   how learnable *my own rubric* is. Its ceiling is set by rubric reliability, which
   is unmeasured.
2. **21 positives.** Every model comparison is noise-limited. The B2 − B3 gap
   (−0.0025) is smaller than fold SD and is correctly described as unresolvable.
3. **No timestamps, no post IDs, no prices.** No return result is possible. This is
   the dominant limitation and is stated in the executive summary.
4. **Views are a final total, not point-in-time.** Even the attention multiplier
   rests on a field that would be look-ahead-biased in a real backtest; it is used
   here only as a descriptive weight on label-recovery analysis.
5. **Truncated text (~265 chars).** Posts are cut off; downstream reasoning may be
   missing.
6. **Theme sparsity: maximum 3 positives per theme.** Per-theme scores are
   indicative orderings, not precise estimates.
7. **Ticker coverage thin: 12 of 19 lexicon tickers never mentioned.**
8. **Near-duplicate detection returned zero clusters.** A true negative here, but
   the deduplication path is untested against a real positive case.
9. **Direction is a fixed annotation input, not a prediction.** 12 up / 7 down / 2
   neutral cannot support a classifier.
